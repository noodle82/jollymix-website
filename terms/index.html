The current edition

JOLLYmix is an interactive Christmas advent calendar for iPhone and iPad, intended for personal, non-commercial entertainment. The 2026 edition contains 24 daily doors, games, toys, companions, jokes, riddles or pep talks, and missions.

The 2026 edition is free. It has no adverts, in-app purchases or subscription. No payment is taken on this website. Any future paid offering would be described with its price and applicable terms before purchase; these terms do not authorise future charges.

Daily doors unlock from 1 to 24 December 2026, just after midnight in the time zone saved during setup. Earlier doors remain available. New-door access needs an online date check. Collected activities remain playable after Christmas and offline. A new calendar for a future year would require a future release; it is not already included in this download.
