Advent Challenge Support

Need a hand with JollyMix Advent Challenge 2026? Email Support@bluffle.co.uk. Include your device model, whether you’re playing in the app or browser, and the door number if it’s relevant.

When can I open the doors?

A new door becomes available each day from 1 to 24 December 2026. Earlier doors stay available. The sample door is ready to try straight away.

Where is the crossword?

Tap the newspaper on the table. The crossword opens on 1 December. The hourglass shows your puzzle times.
