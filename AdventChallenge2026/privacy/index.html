Advent Challenge Privacy

This policy covers JollyMix Advent Challenge 2026, operated by Mark Atkin.

In the app

The app has no account, advertising or third-party analytics. The game does not send us your puzzle answers, times, preferences or progress. They are saved on your device. You can choose to share a result through your device’s share menu.

On the website

The browser game saves progress and settings in your browser’s local storage. As with any website visit, the hosting provider receives the network information needed to serve the page.

Contact and support

Contact Support@bluffle.co.uk or write to Mark Atkin, 30 East Avenue, Bournemouth, BH3 7BZ, United Kingdom. If you email us, we use your message and email address to respond.
