For younger players

Your little world stays on your device.

Use a nickname. We do not need your school, address or birthday to play. Your collection is saved on this device, so people sharing it may see it.

Some toys can listen to your voice or use a picture you choose, with permission. They work on your device; we do not receive your recordings or pictures through the toy.

The app goes online to check which doors can open. If you email us, we can see your email address, message and anything you attach. Ask a trusted adult for help if you like. You can ask us a privacy question too.

Profiles and play on your device

The app stores local profiles or nicknames, surprise collections, game progress and scores, mission completion, joke ratings, care and play choices, and preferences. It also saves the calendar time zone and date-verification information. This allows separate collections on the same device and continued play.

We do not operate a player-account service, online multiplayer or our own cloud progress syncing. The app does not upload this gameplay information to us. Other people using the device may be able to see it. We use the information locally to provide the activities you choose.

Local information remains until it is reset, the app or relevant site data is removed, or the device removes it. Device or cloud backups may contain local app data, depending on your device settings and Apple’s arrangements. We cannot remotely access, recover or erase data held only on your device.

Microphone, pictures and motion

Microphone and voice

Optional activities, including the voice changer and candle, can request microphone permission. Audio is processed locally for the activity. The app does not send your voice to us or a speech-recognition service. Voice recordings used for effects are temporary session data; this release does not provide a recording-upload service.

Pictures

Photo-based play uses the picture you choose on your device. It is not uploaded to us by the toy. If you choose to save or share something through device controls, your chosen service’s terms apply.

Movement

Some toys respond to device movement, such as shaking the fortune ball. Relevant permission may be requested. Movement readings are used for the activity, not uploaded to us or used to build a profile.

You can decline permissions and use other activities. Device settings let you change permissions later. No location permission or GPS position is needed to choose the calendar time zone.

Date checks and website hosting

The app makes an HTTPS request to our time-check origin at jollymix.co.uk to read a server date. This helps prevent a changed device clock from opening future doors. It stores the time-zone name and timing information locally. A time-check request does not intentionally send child profile names, collections, pictures or recordings. A request may include a fresh random value to avoid cached responses; this is not a persistent player identifier.

Opening this website or making a date-check request discloses normal connection information, such as an IP address, request time and technical request details, to the host. This website and the date-check origin use GitHub Pages hosting, as used by BLUFFLE. GitHub states that it logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. Its handling and retention of technical records are described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

We use necessary connection information to deliver and protect the service, on the basis of legitimate interests in operating it securely. Hosting providers also process some information for their own purposes under their notices. We do not operate a separate visitor analytics service or sell personal information.

Cookies and tracking

This public website has no advertising, analytics scripts, social-media embeds, online contact forms or browser-storage code. Fonts and images are served with the site. Its own code does not set cookies. The app uses local storage for the functional information described above; it does not include advertising trackers or an analytics SDK in this release.

Following an App Store, email or other external link contacts another service, whose privacy practices apply. An external link is not the same as embedding that service in this website.

If you contact support

Emailing Support@bluffle.co.uk shares your email address, message, attachments and normal email delivery information with us. Mark Atkin handles support personally. The mailbox uses GoDaddy and Microsoft’s Outlook email service, as for the other BLUFFLE apps.

We use correspondence to answer questions, investigate problems, handle complaints and privacy requests, and keep necessary records. Routine support relies on legitimate interests in providing requested help and maintaining a safe, functioning service. Where a legal obligation requires handling or retention, that obligation is the basis. We take particular care with children’s information and use only what is needed.

We do not use support messages for marketing. Please remove unnecessary personal details from screenshots and never send passwords, payment-card information or unnecessary information about children.

Routine support is kept while an enquiry is open and for up to 12 months after it closes, then deleted or anonymised. This is our existing support-retention policy, not a legally prescribed period. Relevant information may be kept longer where necessary for a specific legal obligation or legal claim, with the reason reviewed. Provider backups or recovery systems may keep deleted messages temporarily under their own arrangements.

Providers, Apple and overseas processing

GitHub hosts website files and the date-check origin; GoDaddy and Microsoft provide email services. Their services may process connection records and correspondence outside the UK, including in the United States. Their applicable privacy notices and data-protection terms explain international-transfer safeguards, including adequacy arrangements or contractual protections with UK-specific terms where required. Contact us for information about safeguards relevant to your correspondence or how to obtain a copy.

See the GoDaddy Privacy Policy, GoDaddy data-processing terms, Microsoft Privacy Statement and Microsoft data-protection terms.

Apple handles App Store downloads and Apple Account information under its own privacy information. When you use TestFlight, Apple automatically collects testing usage and crash information and shares it with the developer. This may include your name and email address if you were invited by email. You cannot opt out of that collection while using TestFlight. Feedback you submit is also shared with the developer. Read Apple’s TestFlight privacy information. This is separate from the app’s local gameplay.

We use developer testing reports to identify and fix problems, relying on legitimate interests in maintaining the app. Any report we export or retain ourselves follows our support-retention policy: while the issue is open and up to 12 months after resolution, unless a specific legal reason requires longer. Apple controls its own platform retention under its notice.

We may disclose relevant information when legally required or as necessary for a legal claim. We do not promise UK-only storage by external providers.

Your privacy rights

You can ask for access, correction, deletion or restriction of personal information we hold and, where applicable, portability. You can object to processing based on legitimate interests: tell us your circumstances and we will consider your request and explain our response. Rights have legal conditions and limits.

Contact Support@bluffle.co.uk or write to the address above. Requests are normally free and answered within one month, subject to lawful exceptions or extensions. We may ask for proportionate information to verify a request. Children have privacy rights too; we can explain these in accessible language and help an appropriate representative.

You can complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office without contacting us first. You can remove local app or browser data using your device controls; removing the app may lose progress. We cannot remotely erase information stored only on your device.

Future changes

We will update this notice and its date if data handling changes. Before introducing accounts, advertising, analytics or materially different processing, we will provide appropriate information and any choices required by law.
