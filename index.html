All questions & support ↗
Can we catch up if we miss a day?

Yes. Once a door unlocks, it stays available. Miss 1 December? You can open that door on 2 December, or any later day. You can also revisit the surprises you have collected.

Does it need the internet?

An internet connection is needed to verify the calendar date before opening new doors, including after restarting the app. Collected games and toys remain available offline. Downloading updates, visiting websites and contacting support also need a connection.

Can we keep playing after Christmas?

Absolutely. Keep enjoying the toys, games and surprises you have collected. The 2026 calendar does not disappear on Christmas Day. We plan a completely new calendar for next year.

Is there a subscription or advertising?

The 2026 edition is free, with no adverts, in-app purchases or subscription. No player account is needed.
