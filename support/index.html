Common questions

What is JOLLYmix?

An interactive Christmas advent calendar for iPhone and iPad. The 2026 edition is Naughty Elf Village: 24 doors, each hiding five little surprises. There are games, playful gifts, jokes, riddles or pep talks, and secret missions.

Can we catch up if we miss a day?

Yes. Once a door unlocks, it stays available. Miss 1 December? You can open that door on 2 December, or any later day. You can also revisit the surprises you have collected.

When do the doors unlock?

Doors open daily from 1 to 24 December 2026. The app detects your device’s time zone when setting up the calendar and remembers it. New doors unlock just after midnight in that saved time zone. The app checks the date online, so changing the device clock does not simply open future doors.

Can more than one child use it?

Yes. Children can have their own local profiles on the same device, with separate collections and progress. Use nicknames. There is no online account or automatic progress syncing between devices.

Does it need the internet?

An internet connection is needed to verify the calendar date before opening new doors, including after restarting the app. Collected games and toys remain available offline. Downloading updates, visiting websites and contacting support also need a connection.

Can we keep playing after Christmas?

Absolutely. Keep enjoying the toys, games and surprises you have collected. The 2026 calendar does not disappear on Christmas Day. We plan a completely new calendar for next year.

Is there a subscription or advertising?

The 2026 edition is free, with no adverts, in-app purchases or subscription. No player account is needed.

Why does it ask for microphone or motion access?

Some optional toys use the microphone, such as the voice changer and blowing out the candle. Some respond to device movement. You can decline these permissions and enjoy the rest of the calendar. Voice effects are processed on your device. See our Privacy Policy for more detail.

Where do we find our collected surprises?

Visit Your little stash to play with collected games and gifts. The Elf Theatre holds collected missions, jokes, riddles and pep talks. There you can mark missions as complete and rate the jokes. When opening a new daily stash, unwrap all five surprises first, then choose what to play.

Where can I download the app?

The public App Store release is coming soon. Open the App Store listing to check availability. If you have already been invited to test, install updates through Apple’s TestFlight app. This website does not provide a public testing invitation.

Quick things to try

A door is still locked

Connect to the internet, reopen the app and allow it to check the date. Doors in this edition unlock from 1 to 24 December 2026 in the time zone saved at setup. A missed door stays available after its unlock date. If it remains locked after that date, contact support.

No sound

Check the app’s sound or effects switch, your device volume and audio settings, and whether sound is going to connected headphones. Try a different game or toy. If only one sound is missing, tell us which one.

A toy cannot hear me

Check microphone permission for JOLLYmix in your device’s Settings. Permission is optional; you can still enjoy other activities without it.

An older version is showing

Update through the App Store, or TestFlight if you are a tester. Refreshing this website does not update the installed app.

Progress is missing

Make sure you selected the same local child profile on the same device. Progress is not automatically shared across devices. Avoid deleting the app as a troubleshooting step: local collections may be lost and we cannot recover them remotely.
